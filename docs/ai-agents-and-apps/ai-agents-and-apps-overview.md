# AI Agents & Apps Overview

**Syskit Point now gives you a unified inventory of the Microsoft agents and apps that can access your Microsoft 365 data.**

:::info

**AI Agents & Apps is currently in Early Access** and still in further development. Available features and their behavior may change as new capabilities are released.

:::

As more people across your organization can create or install agents, it becomes harder to see who is building and using them, and what data they can reach. AI Agents & Apps brings **SharePoint agents, Copilot Studio agents, and Agent Builder agents**, together with the **Entra ID app registrations, enterprise applications, and service principals** in your tenant, into a single, centralized view.

From here you can **review each agent's type, ownership, access, and sensitivity**, **identify orphaned, shared, or sensitive agents**, and **keep your tenant clean** by focusing your governance effort where it matters most.
